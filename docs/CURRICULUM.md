# Aura Music Academy — Curriculum

> **Status.** The 9-level architecture is complete. Lesson **authoring** is an
> ongoing process. This document records exactly what is written and what is
> still planned. Planned lessons are never presented to learners as finished
> work — the UI shows published and planned counts separately.

---

## Level 1 — Music Foundations · සංගීත මූලධර්ම

*For absolute beginners with no musical background.*

### Authored lessons (3 of a planned 12)

| # | Slug | Lesson |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `what-is-music-and-sound` | සංගීතය යනු කුමක්ද? — ශබ්දයේ මූලික ගුණාංග *(Pitch, Rhythm, Tempo, Dynamics, Timbre)* |
| 2 | `musical-alphabet-and-piano-keyboard` | සංගීත හෝඩිය සහ පියානෝ යතුරු පුවරුව *(A–G, sharps/flats, keyboard patterns)* |
| 3 | `staff-notation-rhythm-and-time-signatures` | සංගීත සටහන් කියවීම *(Clefs, note values, 4/4 and 3/4)* |

### Planned lesson slots (9)

Whole tones and semitones introduced practically · note values and rests in depth ·
beat, pulse and subdivision · simple rhythm reading and clapping · octaves and
enharmonic notes · the grand staff and middle C · accidentals and key signatures
(overview) · introduction to notation software · introduction to DAWs.

**Practical outcomes:** identify any note on a keyboard · read basic rhythmic
patterns · play a simple melody on the virtual piano.

## Level 2 — Western Music Theory · බටහිර සංගීත න්‍යාය

### Authored lessons (3 of a planned 12)

| # | Slug | Lesson |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `semitones-major-and-minor-scales` | අර්ධ ස්වර සහ Major–Minor ස්වර මාලා *(T-T-S-T-T-T-S, harmonic/melodic minor, scale degrees)* |
| 2 | `intervals-and-chord-construction` | Intervals සහ කෝඩ් නිර්මාණය *(triads, seventh chords, inversions, slash chords)* |
| 3 | `diatonic-harmony-roman-numerals-and-circle-of-fifths` | Diatonic Harmony, Roman Numerals සහ Circle of Fifths *(functions, cadences, transposition)* |

### Planned lesson slots (9)

Interval quality in depth · key signatures and the circle of fifths analysed ·
cadences in practice · seventh chords in every position · secondary triads ·
modes introduced · ear training for intervals · sight singing basics ·
four-part writing principles · harmonic analysis of a short piece.

**Practical outcomes:** build any major or minor scale · calculate intervals and
chords from formulas · analyse a song with roman numerals.

**Assessment:** 21 quizzes with 71 questions total, covering every authored
lesson. Question types in use: single-choice, multiple-choice, note
identification, chord identification, numeric, checklist and rhythm recognition.

### How these numbers stay honest

- The student dashboard computes progress from `published: true` lessons **at runtime**.
- The learning-paths page renders published counts and planned totals side by side, with a note for every level that still has unauthored slots.
- The integrity test suite fails the build if a lesson references a missing course, prerequisite or quiz; if ids or slugs collide; or if a level lists more lessons than its planned total.
